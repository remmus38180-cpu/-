⚔ Quests

Tasks are quests, organized by realm. Each carries an XP bounty based on priority. Complete quests to level up your guild members.

🏰 Realms

Seven domains organize your work. Click a hex on the board to view and manage quests within that realm.

  • The Forge — Development & engineering
  • Atelier — Design & creative
  • Scriptorium — Documentation & writing
  • Laboratory — Testing & QA
  • Harbor — Deployment & ops
  • Great Hall — Planning & coordination
  • Watchtower — Research & discovery

🎲 Rolling the Bones

Roll two dice to randomly select a realm for focused work. The chosen realm glows gold on the map. Realms with more active quests appear more often.

🏆 Experience & Levels

Completing quests awards XP to the assignee. Accumulate XP to advance through ranks: Apprentice → Journeyman → Artisan → Craftmaster → Warden → Champion → Legendary → Mythic → Ascended.

🤝 Trading

Open the Trading Table to reassign any active quest to a different guild member. Balance workloads to keep the guild thriving.

  • N — New quest
  • R — Roll dice
  • Esc — Close modal
